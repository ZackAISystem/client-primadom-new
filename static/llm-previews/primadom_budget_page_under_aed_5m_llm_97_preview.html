FAQ

Budget questions
What can I buy in Dubai under AED 5M?

Under AED 5M, buyers can usually compare premium apartments, selected 3-bedroom units, townhouses in family communities and some villas depending on district, developer and availability.

Which districts are relevant under AED 5M?

Relevant districts can include Dubai Hills Estate, Business Bay, Dubai Creek Harbour, JVC, Arjan, Meydan, Town Square, Arabian Ranches and Damac Hills depending on property type.

What hidden costs should I check?

Check DLD fees, agency fees, service charges, maintenance, furnishing, mortgage costs, payment plan exposure and possible post-handover obligations.
