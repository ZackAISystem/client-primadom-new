Remote purchase process

Process logic
1. Define buyer goal and budgetInvestment, relocation, rental income, Golden Visa, second home or capital preservation.
2. Verify purchase routeCheck if the buyer will visit Dubai, reserve remotely, use POA or complete steps through developer/advisor process.
3. Prepare documents and payment routeConfirm passport, buyer forms, source-of-funds checks, payment route and developer/bank requirements.
4. Reserve only after live checksCheck current availability, payment plan, project status, handover, fees and legal/admin steps.

FAQ

Origin buyer questions
Can Indian buyers buy property in Dubai?

Indian buyers can generally buy property in Dubai in designated freehold areas, subject to current rules, project status, buyer documents and payment checks.

Can Indian buyers buy Dubai property remotely?

Remote purchase may be possible depending on project, developer, documents, POA requirements, identity checks and payment route. Each case should be checked before reservation.

What documents should Indian buyers prepare?

Typical checks may include passport, contact details, proof of address, payment source, buyer form, reservation documents and any developer or bank-specific requirements.
